Leo (Simha) is a fire sign ruled by the Sun. Leos are known for confidence, warmth and natural leadership. They are generous and enjoy recognition, but pride can turn into stubbornness. The Sun represents the ego, vitality and sense of self in a birth chart.

Aries (Mesha) is a fire sign ruled by Mars. Aries are known for courage, energy and a love of taking the first step. Their strength is drive and fearlessness, but impatience and a quick temper can cause trouble. Mars represents action, energy and willpower in a birth chart.

Sagittarius (Dhanu) is a fire sign ruled by Jupiter. Sagittarius are known for optimism, curiosity and a love of freedom and learning. Their strength is honesty and a wide outlook, but they can be blunt and restless. Jupiter represents growth, wisdom and good fortune in a birth chart.

Fire signs (Aries, Leo and Sagittarius) share energy, enthusiasm and a drive to act. In general they are confident, passionate and inspiring to others, but they can be impulsive, impatient and quick to anger.
